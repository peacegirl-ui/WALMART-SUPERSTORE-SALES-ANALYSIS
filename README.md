# Walmart Sales Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Walmart Sales Performance Dashboard developed using Power BI. The dashboard provides a clear overview of sales, profit, orders, shipping days, regional performance, customer segments, payment methods, and product categories.

The goal of this project is to understand business performance through interactive visuals and identify important sales and profitability trends.

## Dashboard Features

- Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, and Average Ship Days KPIs
- Sales performance by region
- Sales contribution by customer segment
- Sales analysis by payment mode
- Monthly sales and profit trends
- Sales performance by shipping mode
- Sales by product sub-category
- Sales comparison across product categories
- State-wise sales and profit analysis
- Region filters for interactive analysis

## Key Insights

- The dashboard shows overall sales of approximately 2M.
- Total profit is approximately 175K.
- The dashboard includes around 22K orders.
- The average shipping duration shown is 4 days.
- The West region contributes the highest share of sales.
- Consumer customers contribute the largest share among customer segments.
- COD is the leading payment mode in the displayed analysis.
- Standard Class is the most used shipping mode.
- Office Supplies is the highest-performing category by sales.
- Phones, Chairs, and Binders are among the leading sub-categories.
- Monthly sales and profit trends help identify changes in business performance across 2019 and 2020.
